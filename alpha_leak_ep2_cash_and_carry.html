Buy 1 BTC spot and use it as margin to short 1× BTC in the Coin-M delivery future (contract price > spot = contango). At delivery the future settles to spot: the short leg closes at the same price the spot leg bought at, so the position is delta-neutral and you keep the basis = future − spot as risk-free-ish yield (you still carry liquidation risk if the price gaps past your margin). Result shown as APY to the contract's delivery date. All dollar figures are per $1,000 of capital (capital = spot + initial margin) — return % is the same at any size. P&L is net of trading fees: spot buy and sell (2×), futures open (taker) — delivery settlement is free.

How it works

Setup: buy 1 BTC spot @ $S, post it as collateral, short 1 BTC delivery future @ $F (F > S).
At delivery: the future settles to the final spot index. Your short leg closes at exactly the price your spot leg is worth → net price exposure ≈ 0.
You keep: F − S (the basis) minus fees — regardless of where BTC ends up, as long as you survive to delivery.
Risk: if the price gaps up more than your free margin can absorb, the short leg gets liquidated and you're left long-only spot. Initial margin 20% / maintenance 2.5% (tier-1) leaves a wide buffer, but a sustained rally against you is the failure mode — that's the trade-off for the "risk-free" carry.
